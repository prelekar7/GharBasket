# e2e

End-to-end tests. Owners: Rhythm Thakur and Manish Raje.

- **Apps (Playwright):** mobile-web viewport and console. Arrives in `test/e2e-setup`; runs nightly and on PRs labelled `e2e` against the full Docker stack seeded by the simulator. See `docs/handbook/11-engineering-process.md`.
- **Backend: the simulated day (`backend/`, pytest).** `just up && just e2e-day` (a few minutes). On a fresh database of its own (`gharbasket_e2e`, Valkey db 14; created and dropped by the suite), with real commits, the real after-commit outbox relay and the real job endpoints:
  1. migrations: `upgrade head`, `downgrade base`, `upgrade head`, one head, `alembic check` clean;
  2. `just seed` (twice: the second run changes nothing), then `just sim --write-db` for two weeks of synthetic households;
  3. one delivery day (Tuesday T) driven through every job on `main` at its spec's IST time (`POST /v1/internal/jobs/{name}` with the operator token and `scheduled_for`), the process clock moved to each step with `time-machine`: generate (T-3 18:00), box-ready notices (18:30), customers editing, approving, skipping, un-skipping and topping up through the customer endpoints (signed in with a Firebase ID token, as the app does), reminders (T-1 19:00), auto-approve (20:55), the cut-off with a racing second trigger (21:00), the grace period (a top-up that pays, one that falls short, a skip), grace expiry (23:00), the night's housekeeping, packing (T 05:00) and the pushes through a fake FCM, with `outbox_sweep` after each step;
  4. invariants (`backend/invariants.py`, reusable): the ledger balances per entry and across accounts, wallets within 0 and the cap, every order's timeline a legal walk of its kind's state machine, one debit per paid order, cycles agree with their orders, the outbox fully relayed, one message per event and person and none missing, stock equal to its moves (once the stock tables exist).

  `E2E_ML=1` runs it with the ML service up (`just dev-ml`; `E2E_ML_URL`, `E2E_ML_TOKEN`), the simulator then planning with CP-SAT; by default the ML service is down and the fallbacks answer. `E2E_KEEP_DB=1` keeps the database for a look afterwards; `E2E_DATABASE_URL`, `E2E_VALKEY_URL`, `E2E_SIM_SEED`, `E2E_SIM_WEEKS`, `E2E_SIM_ADOPTION` and `E2E_START` change the rest. Product bugs it finds are `xfail(strict=True)` with the bug in the reason, so a fix turns the suite red until the mark goes. Every job on `main` must be in the day or listed in `NOT_IN_THE_DAY` with the reason.
