# Refund, Cancellation and Delivery Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains when you can cancel, how deliveries work, and how and when you get money back. It is part of our [Terms of Service](terms.md). GharBasket is run by [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME].

All times are Indian Standard Time (IST). "Delivery day" means the day your box or tiffin is due to reach you.

## 1. How you pay

- You pay from your **GharBasket wallet**. You add money to it through Razorpay (for example with UPI).
- Your box or tiffin is paid from the wallet at the **cut-off**: **9:00 pm the day before delivery**. Nothing is taken from your wallet before that.
- **UPI Autopay is not offered yet.** The wallet is the only way to pay.

## 2. Cancelling and skipping

**Before the cut-off (9:00 pm the day before delivery)**
- You can skip your weekly box, cancel an approved box, or cancel a tiffin you booked, in the app.
- It is free. No money has left your wallet yet, so there is nothing to refund.
- You can also undo a skip before the cut-off.
- If you do not approve your weekly box and auto-approve is off, no order is made and you are not charged.
- You can pause your subscription. Weeks with a delivery date inside the pause are skipped.

**After the cut-off**
- The order is locked and paid. It can no longer be changed or cancelled, because we buy and pack food for it that night and the next morning.

**If your wallet is short at the cut-off**
- The order waits for payment until **11:00 pm** the same night. We send you a message.
- Top up in that time and the order is paid and goes ahead.
- If it is still unpaid at 11:00 pm, it is cancelled. Nothing is charged.

## 3. Delivery

**Where we deliver**
- We deliver only to housing societies that our hub serves, currently in [PILOT AREA, e.g. Panvel / Kharghar, Navi Mumbai].
- Your address must be in one of these societies.

**When we deliver**
- Your society has fixed delivery days (usually one day a week). The app shows your delivery day and time window, for example "Morning 7–9".
- Tiffins come in the tiffin time slot for that meal (lunch or dinner).

**How we deliver**
- Your bags go to your society's **Society Captain**, a verified partner who lives in or near your society. She brings the bag to your door.
- If your society has no active captain, our rider brings the bag to your door.
- At the door the bag is checked by its QR code. A rider, or anyone delivering a tiffin, asks for your **delivery code** (shown in your app) or takes a photo of the bag at your door.

**Delivery charges**
- If your society is not yet "live" on GharBasket, each weekly box carries a delivery fee of **[PRE-LIVE BOX FEE, default ₹30]**. The app shows it before you approve.
- Once enough households in your society join, the society goes live and boxes are delivered free. If it goes live before your box is paid, the fee is removed.
- Add-ons and tiffins have no delivery fee.

## 4. If you are not home

**Weekly box or add-ons**
1. **Same day:** the captain keeps your bag until **8:00 pm**. You can collect it from her until then.
2. **Next morning:** if you still did not get it, we bring it again once, free, in the same time window.
3. **If the second try also fails:**
   - Fresh items (vegetables, fruit, milk and other items that keep 4 days or less) are refunded at the price you paid, **minus a ₹20 fee**. The refund is never below ₹0.
   - Items that keep longer (such as atta, dal, rice and oil) are **not refunded**. We keep them for you and send them free with your next paid box.
   - If they cannot go with a paid box of yours within **14 days**, we refund them at the price you paid, with **no fee**.

If our rider delivered (no captain) and you were not home, the bag goes back to the hub and the free next-morning delivery above applies.

**Tiffin**
- A tiffin is never brought again. If you are not home, it is **refunded in full** at once.

## 5. Items that are out of stock

- When we pack your box and an item is short, we may send a **similar item at the same price or cheaper**. Any difference is refunded to your wallet at once.
- If only part of an item is short, we refund the missing part.
- You can choose **"Don't replace it, refund me"** in Settings. Then short items are always refunded, never replaced.

## 6. Something wrong with your delivery

Use **Orders → Report a problem** in the app.

- **Missing, damaged, poor quality or wrong item:** report within **48 hours** of delivery.
- **Late or other problems:** report from the time the order leaves the hub until 48 hours after delivery.
- You can report up to 3 problems per order and add up to 3 photos.
- **Instant credit:** a missing item, or a damaged item with a photo, is credited to your wallet at once, as long as your instant credits stay within **₹50 in any 7 days**.
- Everything else is checked by our hub team, who may call you back. They then credit or decline it.
- A damaged item reported without a photo is not declined for that reason; it goes to the hub team.
- The total credited for one order is never more than you paid for it.

**If a delivery fails because of us**
- If a bag or tiffin is damaged or missing at the door, the whole order is **refunded in full** at once.
- If no home kitchen can cook a tiffin you booked, it is **refunded in full** at once, before the meal time.
- If a delivery fails for another reason (for example a wrong address, or the order was refused), our hub team looks at it and either brings it again or refunds it.

We do not take back delivered food. Report a problem instead.

## 7. Where refunds go and how long they take

**Refunds for orders**
- Refunds for orders (shortages, problem credits, failed deliveries) go to your **GharBasket wallet at once**. You can spend them on your next box.
- The wallet can hold at most **₹10,000**. If an order refund would take it over that, the **whole refund goes back to the UPI account or card you paid with**, instead of the wallet.

**Getting your wallet money back ("Refund my balance")**
- Wallet money cannot be withdrawn as cash or sent to another person.
- You can ask for unused wallet money back in the app (**Wallet → Refund my balance**). It goes back **only to the UPI account or card you paid with**.
- Only top-ups from the last **180 days** can take money back, because that is how long Razorpay allows refunds. The app shows how much you can get back now.
- Money is sent back to your newest top-ups first. One refund can be split over several top-ups.
- The smallest refund is ₹1. You can have one refund in progress at a time and ask up to 3 times a day.
- A refund can leave too little in your wallet for a box you already approved. That box will then not be paid at the cut-off (see section 2).

**How long it takes**
- We send each refund request to Razorpay, usually within minutes.
- How long the money then takes to reach your bank account or card is decided by Razorpay and your bank. [REFUND TIME TO BANK, to be confirmed]
- The app shows the status of each refund. If a refund fails, the money comes back to your wallet.

**If you delete your account**
- Your refundable wallet balance is sent back to the UPI account or card you paid with.
- Any part that cannot be sent back (for example, top-ups older than 180 days) is kept safely for you. You can claim it by writing to [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## 8. Payment problems

- **Money left your account but your wallet was not credited:** we check our records against Razorpay every hour and credit missing top-ups automatically. If one still does not show after a few hours, write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the payment time and amount.
- **A payment that arrives late** (after the top-up screen timed out) is still credited if it fits under the ₹10,000 limit. If it does not fit, it is refunded to where it came from.
- **Paid twice by mistake:** our hourly check flags it for our team. Please also write to us.

## 9. Kindness points

Kindness points are not money. They cannot be refunded, cashed out, or used to pay for boxes. They can only be given as a donated meal. They expire 365 days after you earn them.

## 10. Test phase

GharBasket is in an early test with a few housing societies. **Payments are real.** We suggest you start with a small top-up.

## 11. Contact

Questions about a refund or a delivery: [SUPPORT EMAIL], [SUPPORT PHONE], [SUPPORT HOURS]. See [Contact Us](contact.md).
