## 1. Test phase

GharBasket is new. It is being built by a student project team and is now being tested with a small number of housing societies.

- **Payments are real.** Money you add to your wallet is real money.
- Features may change, and some may be paused or removed during the test.

- We suggest you start with a small top-up.

## 2. What GharBasket does

- **Weekly box ("Kitchen Autopilot").** Each week we suggest a meal plan for your household and a box of vegetables, groceries and daily-use items to cook it. You can change, approve or skip it.
- **Add-ons.** You can add extra items to an approved box before the cut-off.
- **Tiffins.** You can book a cooked meal (lunch or dinner) from a verified home kitchen near you.
- **Society Captains.** Bags are delivered to your society and brought to your door by a Society Captain, or by our rider where there is no captain.
- **Kindness.** You can donate items and earn Kindness points, and give those points as a donated meal.
- **Assistant.** You can type to a chat assistant to see or change your week.

## 3. Your account

- You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time password (OTP). You need a working Indian mobile number.
- You must be at least **18 years old** to create an account. You may add family members (by age band) to your household.
- Keep your phone and OTPs private. You are responsible for what happens on your account. You can see your signed-in devices and sign them out in the app.
- One account owns one household. Give a correct society, tower and flat so we can deliver.
- To use the weekly box you must agree to two uses of your data: delivering the service and personalising your plan. Other uses are optional. See the [Privacy Notice](privacy.md).

## 4. The weekly cycle

All times are Indian Standard Time (IST). "T" is your delivery day.

- **Three days before delivery, evening:** your box for the week is ready in the app, and we tell you.
- **You can edit it** (remove, add, change quantities, swap items or meals) until the cut-off.
- **The day before delivery, around 7:00 pm:** if you have not approved yet, we remind you.
- **Cut-off: 9:00 pm the day before delivery.** After this your box cannot be changed, approved, skipped or cancelled.
- **Auto-approve (optional):** if you turn it on, a box you have not approved is approved for you just before the cut-off. This happens only if we told you about the box at least 24 hours before the cut-off.
- If you do not approve and auto-approve is off, no box is sent that week and nothing is charged.
- **Budget Lock (optional):** you can set a monthly budget (at least ₹500). We try to keep boxes within it by choosing cheaper items. If a box still does not fit, we still make it and warn you.
- **Pause:** you can pause your subscription. Weeks inside the pause are skipped.

## 5. Prices and payment

- Prices are in Indian rupees and include [TAX NOTE, e.g. "all applicable taxes" — to be confirmed].
- The price of each box item is fixed when you approve the box. An add-on is priced when you add it. If you edit an approved box before the cut-off, the edited items take the price at that time.
- Your box total includes the delivery fee, if one applies (see the [Refund, Cancellation and Delivery Policy](refunds.md)).
- Your order is paid from your wallet at the cut-off. If your wallet is short, you have until 11:00 pm that night to top up; otherwise the order is cancelled and nothing is charged.
- Prices are set by us. The chat assistant does not set or calculate prices.

## 6. The GharBasket wallet

The wallet is a closed prepaid balance that you can use only on GharBasket.

- **Top-ups** are made through Razorpay. Each top-up is ₹100 to ₹10,000, in whole rupees.
- Your balance can be at most **₹10,000**, and you can top up at most **₹20,000 in a calendar month**.
- A top-up you start but do not pay within 30 minutes expires.
- You **cannot withdraw** wallet money as cash, and you **cannot send it** to another person.
- You can ask for unused money back. It goes back **only to the UPI account or card you paid with**, and only from top-ups made in the last 180 days. See the [Refund, Cancellation and Delivery Policy](refunds.md).
- We never see your card number or UPI PIN. Razorpay handles the payment.
- Your monthly wallet statement is in the app.
- We do not ask customers for KYC documents.

## 7. Kindness points and referrals

- **Kindness points are not money.** They have no cash value. They cannot be used to pay for boxes, added to the wallet, or transferred.
- You can use points only to give a **donated meal** through our partner organisations. One meal takes [KINDNESS GOAL, default 500] points.
- Points expire **365 days** after you earn them.
- **Referrals:** if a neighbour joins with your link and receives their first weekly box, you each earn [REFERRAL POINTS, default 250] points. A referrer earns points for at most 10 referrals in a calendar month.
- **Donations of items:** you earn points only for items our hub accepts and counts. Items the hub cannot use are not returned; they go to a recycler.
