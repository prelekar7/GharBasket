# ADR-0010 — Timeline: plan for April 2027 with a floating buffer sprint

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Decision ID:** D10

## Context
The accepted proposal states about seven months; the idea-presentation form says eight. Semester exam dates and the final submission deadline are not yet known.

## Decision
- Phase 0 (design) runs now → 18 October 2026; Sprint 0 starts 19 October 2026.
- Two-week sprints S0–S12 end on 18 April 2027.
- One **floating buffer sprint** (default 19 April → 2 May 2027) moves to wherever exams fall once dates are published.
- S3 (30 Nov → 13 Dec) is kept light in case exams fall in December.
- Cut order if a milestone slips: voice → OTA updates → UPI Autopay → green-slot pricing → churn/uplift.

## Alternatives considered
- **Compressed March plan** — rejected without a confirmed deadline.
- **May plan** — kept as the buffer rather than the baseline.

## Consequences
- (+) Realistic baseline with explicit slack.
- (−) Must be revisited as soon as exam and deadline dates are known (open question Q1).
