# ADR-0001 — Headline USPs: Kitchen Autopilot and Society Captain

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Decision ID:** D1

## Context
The original documents listed ten or more "unique" features (AI baskets, tiffin, salads, cut-veg kits, women network, charity credits, franchise, community) and claimed competitors had none of them. That message was diluted, and parts were fragile: BB Daily and Country Delight run subscriptions, and quick-commerce players have tried home-style meals. We needed two or three USPs that are defensible, measurable and demo-able, and that match the international models we studied (Picnic's milkman model, China's community group-buying "group leader", Too Good To Go, HelloFresh's churn lessons).

## Decision
Headline two USPs:
1. **Kitchen Autopilot** — AI plans the family's *meals* for the week (fasting, Jain, diabetic, no-cook days) and derives the vegetable box, cut-veg kits and tiffins from that plan; one tap approves the week.
2. **Society Captain ("Ghar Saathi")** — one trained woman per society receives the bulk drop, distributes the last 50 metres, handles returns, crates and donations, and onboards neighbours, earning per-order commission.

Supporting features (built, not headlined): Budget Lock (fixed monthly sabzi bill), zero-waste loop (order-before-we-buy, surplus cascade, return-trip pickups), Kindness Wallet.

## Alternatives considered
- **All four USPs equally** — rejected: the story becomes a feature list and each needs demo-quality polish.
- **Autopilot + Budget Lock** — rejected: strong consumer angle but drops the women-empowerment core of the proposal.
- **Captain + Zero-waste** — rejected: strong SDG story but weak on the "advanced AI" the project promises.

## Consequences
- (+) Clear pitch for viva and investors; the AI and social-impact halves of the proposal are both headlined.
- (+) Society Captain ties women's employment to unit economics (last-mile cost, trust, acquisition), not CSR.
- (−) Autopilot depends on the meal planner, recipe graph and festival calendar — a large Phase 4 effort.
- (−) Captain model needs real recruitment during the pilot.
